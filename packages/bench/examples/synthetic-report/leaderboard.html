Limits

  • Smoothness by the v1 definition only; not validated against human judgement (PLAN D5).
  • Unit of analysis is the brief. Gradients the extractor cannot parse or the scorer skips lower the scorable ratio and are not scored.
  • The brief set is small and written by one team; see docs/bench.md for prompt-set bias.
